# Status

Active

This learning journal is maintained throughout my MSc programme.

It is not part of the submitted assessment.

Its purpose is to capture my thoughts, observations, challenges and learning as they occur so that my final reflections are based on genuine experiences rather than memory.

---

# Purpose

One of the biggest challenges when writing reflective assignments is remembering what actually happened several weeks or months earlier.

Instead of relying on memory at the end of the module, I will capture important learning moments throughout the module.

These notes will later help me prepare:

- discussion reflections
- project reflections
- case studies
- final reflective commentary

---

# What I Record

Whenever something meaningful happens during a module, I will make a short note.

Examples include:

- concepts that were difficult to understand;
- moments where my understanding changed;
- useful feedback from tutors or classmates;
- project decisions;
- technical problems;
- successful solutions;
- mistakes;
- lessons learned.

The notes do not need to be written in perfect academic language.

Their purpose is simply to capture genuine thoughts while they are still fresh.
