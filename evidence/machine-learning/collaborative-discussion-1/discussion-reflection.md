# Collaborative Discussion 1 — Brief Learning Reflection

**What?** My initial post explored Industry 4.0 and Industry 5.0 through the 2022 Rogers telecom outage, focusing on interconnected networks, automation and resilience. I then responded to two healthcare-related discussions about service outages, ransomware and recovery planning.

**So What?** I started with the idea that monitoring and anomaly detection could be important solutions to outages. In my summary post, I recognised that a configuration error can develop into a much larger incident when safeguards, change testing and rollback processes are insufficient. The discussions also showed me that the consequences of technical failures extend to emergency communications and patient care. Monitoring is valuable but it cannot replace people, practical safeguards or clear responsibility.

**Now What?** When working with network automation and machine-learning systems, I want to examine how changes are tested, whether recovery arrangements have been rehearsed and what happens when a model or automated process is wrong. I will also think more carefully about data quality and the people affected by system failures, rather than focusing only on operational efficiency.
