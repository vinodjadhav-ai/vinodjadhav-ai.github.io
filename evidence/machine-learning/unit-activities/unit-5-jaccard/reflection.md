# Unit 5 — Jaccard Distance: Learning Reflection

## What?

In this activity, I calculated the Jaccard Distance between three individuals using their medical test results. I first converted the test results into binary values and then used the Jaccard formula to compare each pair.

## So What?

Before working through this activity, I understood clustering as a method of grouping similar data, but I was not very familiar with how similarity or dissimilarity could be calculated mathematically.

The exercise helped me understand the importance of preparing data before applying a distance measure. I also learned why Jaccard Distance excludes matching zero values and focuses on shared positive attributes and differences between records.

The calculations showed that Jack and Mary were the most similar pair, while Jim and Mary were the most different.

## Now What?

This activity has given me a better understanding of how data characteristics influence the choice of distance measures in machine learning.

I would like to explore how Jaccard Distance could be applied to other datasets, such as comparing features enabled on network devices or identifying similarities between records containing categorical information.

**Learning outcomes addressed:** Understanding the applicability and challenges of different datasets in machine learning.
