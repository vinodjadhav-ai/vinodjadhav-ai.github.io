# Group D — Teamwork and project reflection

## What?

I worked as one member of Group D on the Airbnb NYC market segmentation and price prediction project. My allocated tasks were **data understanding and EDA**. The Trello board provided a shared view of EDA, modelling, clustering, visualisation and report responsibilities. I produced dataset checks, price and category summaries, and graphs that could be used to explain the market before modelling.

## So what?

The project showed me that even when team members work on different technical tasks, their outputs need to agree. For example, the distinction between mean and median price matters in a highly skewed dataset, and an EDA chart needs a clear caption if others are to use it in a report. I also realised that a model may be the strongest among the approaches tested but still explain relatively little variation. The team's Histogram Gradient Boosting model had an R² of 0.146, which means its predictions should be treated as decision support, not automatic pricing instructions.

Trello helped us see responsibilities and upcoming work, although the exported board by itself does not document all meeting decisions or demonstrate completion of every task. I have therefore used it as **coordination evidence**, not as a peer assessment or a replacement for the final report.

## Now what?

In a future group project, I would agree earlier on a shared data dictionary and the meaning of each reported statistic; document data-cleaning decisions in a single place; and keep a short decision log so modelling and reporting teams can trace choices back to exploratory results. I would also share intermediate visuals with captions rather than waiting until report assembly.
