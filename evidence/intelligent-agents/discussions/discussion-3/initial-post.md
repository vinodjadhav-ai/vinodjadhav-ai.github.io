## Topic

Ethical Issues in Deep Learning

## Overview

My initial discussion explored the ethical challenges associated with modern deep learning systems. Rather than focusing only on technical performance, I discussed how fairness, transparency, explainability and human oversight should be considered throughout the AI lifecycle.

## Main Points

- Bias introduced through training data
- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Trust and accountability
- Human oversight
- Responsible deployment
- Transparency

## Personal Learning

Before studying this topic, I mainly viewed deep learning as a powerful technical solution. Through researching this discussion, I realised that high model accuracy alone does not guarantee fairness, trust or responsible behaviour. Ethical considerations should therefore be treated as core design requirements rather than optional additions.
