# Collaborative Discussion 1 — Feedback Received

## Overview

During Collaborative Discussion 1, I received constructive feedback from two classmates. Their comments helped me consider additional perspectives on agent-based systems and encouraged me to think more critically about both the advantages and limitations of autonomous agents.

---

## Feedback 1

### Main points raised

- Appreciated my explanation of why agent-based systems have become important.
- Agreed with the networking examples that I used.
- Suggested that I also discuss the risks of autonomous decision-making.
- Highlighted that human oversight remains important.

### What I learned

This feedback reminded me that intelligent agents should not only be evaluated by their technical capabilities but also by their reliability, accountability and ethical implications.

---

## Feedback 2

### Main points raised

- Appreciated the practical examples used in the discussion.
- Suggested including more discussion about collaboration between multiple agents.
- Recommended expanding the explanation of ethical and governance issues.

### What I learned

This feedback encouraged me to look beyond individual intelligent agents and understand how multiple agents cooperate within larger systems. It also reinforced the importance of considering governance alongside technical implementation.

## Personal Reflection

Receiving feedback from classmates helped me view the topic from different perspectives. Rather than simply defending my original ideas, I found it useful to compare different viewpoints and strengthen my own understanding before writing the Summary Post.
