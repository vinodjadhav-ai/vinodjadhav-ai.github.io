# Collaborative Discussion 1 — My Peer Responses

## Overview

As part of Collaborative Discussion 1, I responded to two classmates' initial posts. My aim was not simply to agree with their opinions, but to extend the discussion by connecting their ideas with module concepts and relevant literature.

---

## Response to Peer 1

### Discussion focus

- Agent autonomy
- Human oversight
- Ethical deployment

### My contribution

I agreed that autonomous agents provide many organisational benefits, but also highlighted that increasing autonomy requires appropriate governance, transparency and accountability. I supported the discussion using ideas from Wooldridge (2009) and Russell and Norvig (2021).

### Learning gained

Writing this response improved my confidence in discussing both technical and ethical aspects together rather than treating them separately.

---

## Response to Peer 2

### Discussion focus

- Large Language Models
- Agent communication
- Emerging AI technologies

### My contribution

I extended the discussion by explaining that Large Language Models may enhance intelligent agents through improved reasoning and natural language interaction, but should still operate within clearly defined constraints.

### Learning gained

This discussion helped me appreciate that emerging AI technologies introduce both new opportunities and additional responsibilities for developers.

Responding to classmates required me to read their arguments carefully before forming my own response. This improved my academic communication skills and helped me become more comfortable participating in scholarly discussions.
