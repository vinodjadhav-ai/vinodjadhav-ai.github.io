# Team Project Evidence

This folder contains the supporting evidence for the **Team Project** section of my Intelligent Agents e-Portfolio.

The Team Project formed the design phase of **Inquiro**, a multi-agent academic research assistant developed by Team C. Rather than reproducing the complete Team Design Proposal, this folder provides evidence that demonstrates my participation, learning and reflection throughout the project.

Contents include:

- A summary of the team planning meeting.
- A reflection on teamwork and collaboration.
- A technical reflection on the project design and how it influenced my individual implementation.
- Selected screenshots of the proposed architecture, workflow and meeting summary.

Together these artefacts support the narrative presented in the e-Portfolio and demonstrate my contribution to the team design process while maintaining the focus on my own learning and professional development.
