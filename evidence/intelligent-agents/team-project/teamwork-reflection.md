# Teamwork Reflection

Working on the Team Project gave me valuable experience of collaborating within a distributed software development team. Although team members were working from different locations and managing different schedules, regular communication through online meetings and messaging helped maintain progress throughout the project.

One of the strengths of the team was that members contributed different perspectives and technical skills. Discussions around the proposed architecture, technology stack and implementation approach were constructive and encouraged deeper analysis of the design rather than accepting the first solution. This reinforced the importance of listening to different viewpoints before making technical decisions.

From my own perspective, I contributed by participating in discussions, reviewing technical ideas, asking questions about the proposed architecture and helping document meeting outcomes. I also tried to support collaboration by sharing feedback respectfully and remaining focused on the overall objectives of the project.

The experience highlighted several areas for my future development. I would like to become more confident when making technical decisions and continue improving my software engineering knowledge so that I can contribute more effectively during architecture discussions. I also learned that recording decisions and agreed actions immediately after meetings helps improve transparency and accountability throughout the project.

Overall, this activity strengthened my understanding of collaborative software development and demonstrated that successful projects depend on communication and teamwork as much as technical ability.
