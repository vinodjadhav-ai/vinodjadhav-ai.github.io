# Unit 10 Reflection

## What?

This activity introduced me to a practical application of deep learning through image recognition using Convolutional Neural Networks. I explored how deep learning models learn patterns from large datasets and how they are used in real-world systems.

## So What?

Before completing this activity, I understood neural networks mainly from a theoretical perspective. Investigating a practical application helped me appreciate why deep learning has become so successful in fields such as healthcare, autonomous vehicles and computer vision.

The activity also changed the way I think about AI systems. I realised that achieving high accuracy is only one part of building a successful AI solution. Issues such as privacy, fairness, transparency and accountability are equally important because AI systems can directly affect people's lives.

Understanding these ethical considerations helped me recognise that responsible AI development requires both strong technical design and careful governance.

## Now What?

In future modules and projects, I will pay greater attention to both the technical and ethical aspects of AI systems.

I would also like to learn more about Explainable AI (XAI), bias mitigation techniques and methods for evaluating deep learning models to ensure they remain reliable, fair and trustworthy.

Overall, this activity strengthened my understanding of deep learning and reinforced the importance of developing AI systems responsibly.
